          ↓
2. Add Guitar to Cart
          ↓
3. Checkout
          ↓
4. Check Inventory
          ↓
5. Create Order
          ↓
6. Calculate Total Amount
          ↓
7. Select Payment Method
          ↓
8. Process Payment
          ↓
9. Payment Success
          ↓
10. Reduce Inventory
          ↓
11. Update Order = Paid
          ↓
12. Send Order Confirmation


For the Guitar Purchase System, I would start with Product, Customer, Cart, Order, OrderItem, Inventory and Payment entities. For payment methods such as Card and UPI, I would use Strategy Pattern, and Factory Pattern can create the appropriate payment strategy. I would use Repository Pattern for data access and Dependency Injection for loose coupling. The purchase flow would be Cart → Order → Inventory validation → Payment → Order confirmation. If the system needs to scale, I can separate Catalog, Order, Payment and Inventory into services and use Kafka for asynchronous events.
